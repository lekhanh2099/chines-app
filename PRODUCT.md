# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent Chinese learners using HanziHome for focused lesson study, vocabulary
review and content editing across desktop, iPad and mobile. They need to scan a
lesson quickly, understand one word deeply and move between words without losing
context.

## Product Purpose

HanziHome turns structured Chinese course content into a practical study
workspace. Success means learners can identify the current word, pronunciation,
meaning, usage and learning state immediately, then explore deeper material only
when needed.

## Brand Personality

Clear, focused and modern. The interface should feel calm and dependable during
long study sessions, with enough character to support Chinese learning without
decorative noise.

## Anti-references

- Decorative glass layers, excessive purple tinting or weak tinted contrast.
- Oversized empty surfaces that push examples and usage below the fold.
- Nested cards that make every detail look equally important.
- Non-standard controls, hidden actions or typography settings that do not
  affect the content.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current word, pronunciation and primary meaning first.
2. Reveal depth progressively while keeping common study actions close.
3. Use color for selection, learning state and feedback, not decoration.
4. Preserve one interaction vocabulary across list, detail, drawer and editor.
5. Keep study flow fast with keyboard navigation and responsive reading layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, touch-friendly targets,
reduced-motion support and layouts that remain usable on iPad portrait and
mobile. Chinese text controls must be labelled and their selected font must
visibly affect the rendered Hanzi.
